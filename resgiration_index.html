NEED HELP?

Frequently asked questions

Is registration free for MIT students?

According to the current information provided, the application is free for MIT students.

When do external applicants pay the fee?

Any applicable fee is communicated after approval. Do not pay during form submission unless the centre publishes a different approved process.

Does registration guarantee funding?

No. Funding depends on the scheme, eligibility requirements, selection process and availability.

Can I apply if my startup is not registered?

The form supports unregistered startups, but final incubation and scheme eligibility depends on the centre's policy.
