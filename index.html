About Us

Where student ideas find support

We are the startup and innovation hub of MITGI. Our goal is to build an entrepreneurial culture on campus and help students and alumni move from a rough idea to a working product and a registered venture.

  • ✓ Mission: nurture innovators and job creators
  • ✓ Open to students, faculty and alumni
  • ✓ Connected with industry, investors and government schemes

What We Offer

Everything a young startup needs

Mentorship

One-on-one guidance from founders, industry experts and faculty.

Workspace & Labs

Co-working desks, prototyping tools and meeting rooms on campus.

Funding Support

Seed grants, pitch days and introductions to angels and VCs.

Training & Workshops

Business planning, pitching, finance, marketing and legal basics.

Legal & IP Help

Support with company registration, trademarks and patent filing.

Industry Network

Pilot projects, internships and partnerships with companies.

How It Works

From idea to incubation in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Apply

    Submit your idea and team details online.

  2. 2

    Screening

    Pitch to our committee of mentors and faculty.

  3. 3

    Incubate

    Get mentors, workspace and build your MVP.

  4. 4

    Launch & Scale

    Demo day, funding access and market launch.
