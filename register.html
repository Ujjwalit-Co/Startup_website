Who Can Apply?

Nirman Incubation Centre welcomes aspiring entrepreneurs who want to transform their innovative ideas into successful startups. Whether you are a student of MIT or an external applicant, you can apply for incubation support as per the centre's eligibility criteria.

1. MIT Students

Empowering Student Innovators

Are you an MIT student with a startup idea or an entrepreneurial vision? Apply to Nirman Incubation Centre to explore opportunities for mentorship, workspace, technical support, and potential funding assistance.

  • Open to MIT students with startup ideas or ventures.
  • Application Fee: Free.
  • Access to incubation opportunities, subject to approval and availability.

2. External Applicants

Welcoming Entrepreneurs Beyond MIT

Are you a student from another institution, a working professional, or an independent entrepreneur? Nirman Incubation Centre welcomes eligible external applicants who are passionate about building innovative and scalable startups.

  • Open to eligible students, working professionals, and freelancers.
  • Application Fee: Applicable as per the approved fee policy.
  • Incubation and mentorship opportunities, subject to eligibility and approval.

Important Note:- No payment is required during registration. External applicants will receive the applicable fee details and payment instructions after approval. Incubation space and funding are subject to availability, eligibility, and the centre's policies.

Startup Eligibility And Stages

1. Idea Stage

You have identified a problem and are developing your proposed solution.

2. Prototype Stage

You are testing your idea or building an early version of your product.

3. MVP Stage

You have developed a minimum viable product and are validating it with users.

4. Revenue Stage

Your startup is operating and generating revenue.

Documents Required

Document Applicant Type Requirement
College ID Card MIT Students Required
ID Proof External Applicants Required
DPIIT Certificate Recognized Startups When applicable
Pitch Deck All Applicants Optional

Important Note:-Keep the relevant documents ready before starting your application. The documents required may vary according to applicant type and the support or funding being requested.
